# Jaguar–Shadow mesh match review — 2026-10-14

**Decision:** Jaguar and Shadow's silhouettes were accepted for the prototype
blockout milestone on 2026-10-14. This review records match presentation and
remaining motion work; it is not approval of final surfacing or a complete
animation set.

## What runs in the prototype

The [Jaguar](../robots/jaguar/blockout/README.md) and
[Shadow](../robots/shadow/blockout/README.md) editable glTF blockouts share
their source geometry with `project/prototype/model_meshes.h`, generated by
`project/tools/generate_model_meshes.py`. The prototype renders those
triangles and maps each named rigid part to shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip,
knee, and ankle poses. Source OpenOMF state still owns position, facing,
health, hit, block, and move timing. The model transform is presentation only.

The current pose set includes idle sway, walking stride, jump tuck, standing
guard, punch, kick, hit recoil, and one signature pose sequence for each HAR.
Jaguar punch (animation 42), kick (41), and down punch (30), plus Shadow kick
(42) and punch (43), use the existing source-sprite pose catalog. Jaguar's
concussion-cannon animation 21 and Shadow's Shadow Punch animation 23 now
have sprite-keyed arm poses. The [signature move trace](OMF2115_SIGNATURE_MOVE_TRACE.md)
records both at near and distant spacing. The source frames were exported locally and
matched to the corresponding higher-resolution Remastered atlas frames by
pixel fingerprint. The approved OMF2115 three-view sheets define the meshes;
the two game sources guide motion only. Each robot also has a portable
rigid-joint glTF with eight editor preview clips. Those clips are useful for
asset review; source-sprite pose selection in the prototype remains the
match presentation path. Neither glTF is a final skinned deformation rig.

`build/omf_trace_z7/6p-trace.csv`. The prototype displayed its completed
source ticks through `--source-replay`. The local captures show
[neutral tick 125](../build/mesh-match-ticks/original-125.bmp),
[Jaguar's first punch at tick 128](../build/mesh-match-ticks/original-128.bmp),

while Shadow remains at x=248.205. The punches remain visibly short in the 3D
view. Facing stays Jaguar right, Shadow left in these sampled exchanges.

The source punch range sweep gives a more demanding reach check. At tick 128
Jaguar is at x=66.8. Shadow at x=154.0 (87.2 source units apart) loses 7 HP;
Shadow at x=155.0 (88.2 apart) is not hit. The corresponding local prototype
captures are [hit at the range boundary](../build/mesh-match-ticks/range-154-tick-128.bmp)
and [miss one unit farther](../build/mesh-match-ticks/range-155-tick-128.bmp).
The source-view X mapping and camera magnification were adjusted together so
fighter centers retain approximately the same screen positions and apparent
movement speed while the model silhouettes occupy more of the original
sprite's width. Jaguar's extended hand now meets Shadow's outline in the
range-boundary hit capture. The one-unit-farther miss looks nearly identical
at this scale, as expected for such a small input change. In the close hit
capture, however, Jaguar's hand passes visibly through Shadow. **Reach is
readable at the sampled maximum, but close-contact posing remains inaccurate.**
The renderer still leaves the OpenOMF hit result untouched. A later
contact-pose pass folds the striking arm toward a nearby opponent; the
[updated close punch](../build/mesh-match-ticks/punch-close-after.bmp)
reaches Shadow's near side while the
[unchanged-distance punch](../build/mesh-match-ticks/punch-far-after.bmp)
retains its long silhouette. These are local review captures in `project/build/`.

Jaguar's kick has the same timing relationship. In the unchanged
[`kick-trace.csv` tick 129](../build/mesh-match-ticks/kick-trace-129.bmp),
the extended leg misses at the original spacing. The local
[`kick-hit-trace.csv` tick 129](../build/mesh-match-ticks/kick-hit-trace-129.bmp)
places Shadow at x=120.0; its health falls from 196 to 177 on that tick and
the foot reaches the lower torso. The closer
[`kick-close-trace.csv` tick 129](../build/mesh-match-ticks/kick-close-trace-129.bmp)
also records 177 HP, but the foot passes behind Shadow's center in the 3D

The contact-pose pass likewise folds the raised leg at close range. In the
[updated close kick](../build/mesh-match-ticks/kick-close-after.bmp), the
foot stops near Shadow's near side. The
[updated wider hit](../build/mesh-match-ticks/kick-hit-after.bmp) keeps a
longer leg and the same source result of 177 HP. This is a visual fit for the
sampled contacts, not a full move-range validation.

## Close-contact body spacing

The limb folds reduced fist and foot pass-through, but the two blockout
silhouettes still overlapped at the 29.2-source-unit close fixtures. The source
replay renderer now applies a symmetric, reach-weighted horizontal offset to
the *meshes only* during supported close-contact poses, up to 1.20 prototype
world units of visual center spacing. It uses that rendered spacing for the
punch/kick limb fold. OpenOMF positions, hit timing, damage, projectiles, and
replay data are unchanged.

## Jaguar down-punch trace

The actual source trace for animation 30 confirms Jaguar's down punch reaches
Shadow at close range: from a 29.2-unit starting gap, Shadow loses 19 HP on
tick 129 (animation tick 5, sprite 2). Source state remains authoritative for
the hit. The 3D pose now uses the same opponent-distance fold for Jaguar's
down-punch arm as for its standing punch, reducing the long-limb pass-through
at close contact. Compare the local [before](../build/omf3d_jag_down_punch_tick129.png)
and [after](../build/omf3d_jag_down_punch_contact_tick129.png) fight-camera
captures. This is a single contact sample; see the [Jaguar baseline](OMF2115_JAGUAR_BASELINE.md)
for the trace timeline and remaining recovery measurement.

## Shadow Punch animation

The first source-driven Shadow Punch view changed the arm joints across
animation 23 but left the torso and legs in their neutral stance, making the
move appear static. The current pass keys a forward body lean and wide lunge
from the recorded source sprite, plus a cyan hand flash during sprites 0–2.
The projection still spawns and travels only from OpenOMF's recorded projectile
objects. Local startup, active, projection-spawn, and near/far hit captures are
linked in the [signature move trace](OMF2115_SIGNATURE_MOVE_TRACE.md).

## Remaining work

1. Validate standing and down-punch plus kick contact through complete move
   traces and live play; the sampled Jaguar contacts now fold visually without
   changing source hit results. The down-punch recovery tail remains unmeasured.
2. Refine the source-positioned concussion shot and Shadow projection
   blockouts, including near Shadow Punch contact. The
   [signature move trace](OMF2115_SIGNATURE_MOVE_TRACE.md) records their travel.
3. Human-review the close-contact separation, accepted side silhouettes, and
   signature moves at normal speed through a full live match. The source replay
   captures verify geometry placement at sampled contact ticks but do not
   replace an OMF-familiar playtest.
4. For the finished asset pass, replace rigid-part motion with skinned
   deformation rigs and refine clip timing against full source move traces.

All original-game and Remastered reference images remain in ignored local
folders. The glTF models and generated render data include only new blockout
geometry and flat colors.
